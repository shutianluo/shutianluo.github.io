Jie Zhang is a master's student in the AI Infra Group at the University of Macau. He received his B.S. in Mechanical Electronics Engineering from North University of China. Before joining the University of Macau, he spent six years at Gree Electric Appliances as a Team Lead of Control Technology R&D, where he led the design and delivery of control firmware and display systems for consumer appliances and holds several granted patents.
