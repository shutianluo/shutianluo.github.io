Renjie Cai is a Ph.D. student in the AI Infra Group at the University of Macau. He received his B.S. in Cyberspace Security from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, where he worked as a research assistant on machine learning systems. His work spans efficient serving systems for vision-language-action (VLA) models on edge GPUs, profiling Transformer workloads on NPUs, and secure inference in trusted execution environments (TEEs).
