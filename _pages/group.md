I lead the **AI Infra Group** in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Macau. We build efficient systems and infrastructure for large-scale AI over emerging heterogeneous computing architectures, with a current focus on efficient LLM systems, CPU–GPU superchips, and physical AI.
